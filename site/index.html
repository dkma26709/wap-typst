Warhammer Armies Project

32 armies and the core rulebook, 36 books between them, 1,816 unit entries, re-typeset with Typst.

Some books have an edition of our own beside the original: the house rules we play, and the proposals we are still arguing about. There are 11 of them, on a shelf of their own. What an edition changed - and what it only proposes - is set out in the back of it.

These are unofficial, non-commercial re-typesettings of the Warhammer Armies Project army books, written and freely distributed by Mathias Eliasson. All rules text, army design and points values remain the work of their author; only the typesetting differs.

Editions marked as modified are the exception: those alter the rules text itself. The alterations are ours, not the author's, and each book lists them in full.
